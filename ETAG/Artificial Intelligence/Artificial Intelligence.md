## 🎯 High-Yield GATE Topics (Priority Order)

2. **Alpha-Beta Pruning** — Cutoff conditions, move ordering, complexity
3. **d-Separation** — Chain/Fork/V-structure, explaining away

5. **Resolution** — Refutation completeness, CNF conversion
6. **FOL Unification** — MGU, occurs check, Skolemization
