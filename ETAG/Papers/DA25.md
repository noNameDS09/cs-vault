## **Q 16**
### Concept
A decomposition is **dependency-preserving** if all functional dependencies can be checked **without joining** the decomposed relations.

If it is **not dependency-preserving**, the original relation must be reconstructed using a **JOIN** to verify or enforce dependencies.

### Answer

**(C) Join**

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\text{(C) Join}}
$$

## **Q.17**

### Given Expression

$$
\pi_{\text{owner}}
\left(
\text{Own}
\bowtie
\left(
\sigma_{\text{color}="red"}
\left(
\text{Car}
\bowtie
\left(
\sigma_{\text{maker}="ABC"}(\text{Make})
\right)
\right)
\right)
\right)
$$

### Step-by-step

1. Select maker = "ABC":
   $$
   \sigma_{\text{maker}="ABC"}(\text{Make})
   $$
   → Models made by **ABC**.

2. Natural join with `Car`:
   $$
   \text{Car} \bowtie \text{Make}
   $$
   → Cars made by **ABC**.

3. Select red cars:
   $$
   \sigma_{\text{color}="red"}
   $$
   → **Red cars made by ABC**.

4. Natural join with `Own`:
   $$
   \text{Own} \bowtie (\cdots)
   $$
   → Owners of those cars.

5. Project owner:
   $$
   \pi_{\text{owner}}
   $$
   → Return only owner names.

### Answer

Owners of **red cars made by ABC**.

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\text{(C) All owners of a red car made by ABC}}
$$
