### Step 1: What is an admissible heuristic?

A heuristic $h(n)$ is **admissible** if it **never overestimates** the true cost to reach the goal.

If $h^*(n)$ is the true optimal cost, then

$$  
0 \le h(n) \le h^*(n)  
$$

Since $h_1$ and $h_2$ are admissible,

$$  
0 \le h_1 \le h^*  
$$

and

$$  
0 \le h_2 \le h^*  
$$

---

### Step 2: Check each option

#### (A) $h_1 + h_2$

Not always admissible.

Example:

- $h^* = 10$
    
- $h_1 = 8$
    
- $h_2 = 7$
    

Then

$$  
h_1 + h_2 = 15 > 10  
$$

It overestimates the true cost.

❌ Not admissible.

---

#### (B) $h_1 \times h_2$

Not always admissible.

Example:

- $h^* = 4$
    
- $h_1 = 4$
    
- $h_2 = 4$
    

Then

$$  
h_1h_2 = 16 > 4  
$$

❌ Not admissible.

---

#### (C) $\dfrac{h_1}{h_2}$

Not always admissible.

Example:

- $h_1 = 4$
    
- $h_2 = 1$
    
- $h^* = 5$
    

Then

$$  
\frac{h_1}{h_2} = 4  
$$

This is admissible in this example, but consider:

- $h_1 = 2$
    
- $h_2 = 0.1$
    
- $h^* = 3$
    

Then

$$  
\frac{2}{0.1} = 20 > 3  
$$

❌ Not admissible.

---

#### (D) $|h_1 - h_2|$

Since

$$  
0 \le h_1, h_2 \le h^*  
$$

the maximum possible difference between them cannot exceed $h^*$.

Hence,

$$  
|h_1 - h_2| \le h^*  
$$

Therefore, it never overestimates the true cost.

✅ Always admissible.

---

## GATE Shortcut

If $h_1$ and $h_2$ are admissible:

- ❌ $h_1 + h_2$ → can overestimate
    
- ❌ $h_1 \times h_2$ → can overestimate
    
- ❌ $\dfrac{h_1}{h_2}$ → can overestimate
    
- ✅ $|h_1 - h_2|$ → **always admissible**

> If $h_1$ and $h_2$ are admissible, then
> $$  \max(h_1, h_2)  $$

### Q.40 Binary Search Recurrence

**Answer:** **(A)**

In binary search:
- One comparison is made at the middle element.
- The search then continues in **one half** of the array (the larger half in the worst case).

Hence, the recurrence relation is:

$$
F(n) = F\left(\left\lfloor \frac{n}{2} \right\rfloor\right) + 1
$$

where:
- `+1` = comparison at the current level.
- $\left\lfloor \frac{n}{2} \right\rfloor$ = size of the remaining search space.

**Final Answer:** **(A)**
